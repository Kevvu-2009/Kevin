## Cottages
- **Koivu** — 4 guests, 1 bedroom + sleeping loft, wood-heated sauna, rowing boat.
- **Mänty** — 6 guests, 2 bedrooms + sleeping loft, wood-heated sauna, rowing boat, dishwasher.
- **Kuusi** — 8 guests, 3 bedrooms, electric + wood-heated sauna, hot tub (extra fee), dishwasher.
- All cottages: electricity, running water, indoor toilet and shower, Wi-Fi, fully equipped
  kitchen, barbecue grill, outdoor furniture, dock.

## Prices (2026, per night, incl. VAT)
- Koivu: 120 € (min. 2 nights)
- Mänty: 160 € (min. 2 nights)
- Kuusi: 220 € (min. 3 nights)
- Midsummer week and Christmas–New Year: weekly booking only; price on request from the owner.
- Final cleaning: 80 € (or guests clean themselves).
- Bed linen and towels: 20 € per person.
- Hot tub (Kuusi only): 90 € per stay.
- Pets: allowed in Koivu and Mänty only, 30 € per pet per stay. Max 2 pets.

## FAQ
- **Is there a shop nearby?** Yes, a grocery store 6 km away.
- **Can we fish?** Yes. Guests aged 18–69 need the national fishing fee (kalastonhoitomaksu).
- **Is smoking allowed?** Not indoors. Smoking is allowed outdoors away from the cottage.
- **Is there parking?** Free parking for 2 cars next to each cottage.
- **Do you have a charger for electric cars?** No.
- **Is the road open in winter?** Yes, the road is ploughed. Winter tyres are required.
